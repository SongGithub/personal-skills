# Job Evaluation Framework

## Scoring Dimensions (1-10)

### Must-Haves (weight 3x)
1. **Tech Stack Fit** — Kubernetes, Terraform, AWS, CI/CD? Are they real production users or "we'll migrate soon"?
2. **Seniority Alignment** — Senior/Lead IC or Lead hybrid? Not junior/mid levels
3. **Platform Engineering Philosophy** — Do they treat infra as a product or as tickets?
4. **Impact Scope** — Will I own meaningful platform decisions or just execute tickets?

### Nice-to-Haves (weight 2x)
5. **AI/Agentic Tooling** — Do they allow/encourage AI tooling (Claude Code, Copilot, etc.)?
6. **Domain Match** — Direct match for the candidate's prior industry experience
7. **Team Culture** — Strong DevOps practice, SRE maturity, blameless postmortems
8. **Growth Potential** — Can I level up into Staff/Principal track?

### Stretch Factors (weight 1x)
9. **Compensation** — Total comp vs market rate for equivalent seniority
10. **Commute / WFH** — hybrid or remote-friendly in the candidate's city
11. **Tech Stack Breadth** — Opportunity to work with new tech (e.g. more Go, more AI infra)
12. **Company Stage** — Growth-stage vs mature — how much platform work is genuinely needed

## Minimum Thresholds

### Must Pass
- Tech stack fit >= 7/10
- Seniority alignment >= 7/10
- Total score >= 70/120 (weighted)

### Dealbreakers (automatic no)
- No Kubernetes in production
- "DevOps" = Ops with no IaC/platform charter
- AI tooling banned on corporate devices
- On-call without SRE practices (no incident analysis, no alert tuning)
- Below-market compensation (>20% under market for senior platform role)
- Relocation required outside the candidate's city
