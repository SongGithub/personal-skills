# 20-Year Career Design from First Principles

*A career-strategy framework for long-horizon role selection.*
*Use this as the primary strategic filter for all job-seeking decisions.*

---

## The Core Equation

**Career value = (how scarce the problem you solve) × (how many need it) ÷ (how many others can solve it)**

A "thriving career" = numerator rising & denominator falling.
A "dying career" = denominator rising (more people/AI can do it) & numerator falling (problem standardised).

---

## Five Axioms

### Axiom 1: AI splits work into Pile A (known-answer execution) and Pile B (judgment/creation/leverage)
- Pile A → commodity, cost toward zero
- Pile B → judgment, design, trust, orchestration — AI *amplifies* people here
- **Rule:** Every 2-3 years, ask: "Can a competent person + today's AI do most of this from documentation?" If yes → drifted into Pile A, move up.

### Axiom 2: Build leverage, never sell hours
1. **Linear** — you do work, get paid once. Caps at hours. Dies first.
2. **Leverage** — build once, N reuse forever. (Platforms, tools, golden paths.)
3. **Compounding** — leverage builds reputation + network + assets that generate opportunity *without you asking*. (Known name, track record, owned IP, business.)

### Axiom 3: Skills decay; meta-skills compound
- **Skills** (tools, clouds, frameworks) have a half-life.
- **Meta-skills** never decay: learn fast, judgment under ambiguity, influence without authority, translating business → technical leverage, building trust.
- **Rule:** Weight each role partly by "what durable meta-skill does this force me to build?" — not just "does it use my current skills?"

### Axiom 4: Ride curves, don't cling to peaks
- DevOps was rising ~2014; flattening now.
- AI/agentic platforms = steep rising curve NOW. Being early matters.
- ~5-7 years, agentic AI matures. The skill is not "AI" — it's spotting & boarding the *next* curve.
- **Rule:** Edge is being the person who operationalises whatever the next expensive workload is.

### Axiom 5: Optimise environment, not just role
- **Profit-centre > cost-centre** — where engineering IS the product, you're an investment.
- **Rising company on a rising curve** — hiring for capability vs exact-match commoditisation.
- **Rule:** A great role in a dying environment is still a dying job.

## The 20-Year Arc

### Phase 1 (now → ~5 yrs): Become the rare "builds AND influences at the AI frontier"
- **Goal:** Convert scarce technical asset (production agent platforms) into *recognised* leverage, build influence/judgment meta-skill.
- **Right roles:** platform / AI-enablement / platform-as-a-product roles at profit-centre companies.
- **Protect:** Stay hands-on; keep boarding AI curve deeper (orchestration, evals, governance, agent infra).
- **Meta-skill:** Influence without authority + translating business → technical leverage.

### Phase 2 (~5-12 yrs): Become a multiplier of multipliers
- **Goal:** Lead platform/AI capability across an org, or productise own approach.
- **Forks:** Senior IC (Staff/Principal/Distinguished), eng leadership, or own AI consultancy.
- **Meta-skill:** Strategy, org-level influence, commercial judgment.

### Phase 3 (~12-20 yrs): Own the compounding asset
- Reputation, network, IP generate opportunity *for* you.

## Decision Filter — Score Every Role 0-2 on Each

1. **Pile B?** Does it create/leverage, or execute known answers?
2. **Rising curve?** Is the underlying tech/skill ascending?
3. **Profit-centre + growing?** Is engineering the product, in a rising company?
4. **Builds a meta-skill?** Does it force durable growth (influence, judgment)?
5. **Moves up the leverage ladder?** Linear → leverage → compounding?
6. **Keeps hands on the frontier?** Will my technical edge sharpen, not fade?

**Red flags (any one = serious caution):**
- "Keep the lights on" / BAU / ticket execution as core
- Cost-centre overhead function
- Skill that AI/managed services are actively absorbing
- Pure linear value (paid per hour, no leverage built)
- Flattening-curve technology as main bet

---

## The Steering Sentence

> **"Am I moving toward being the person who builds the leverage on the next rising curve in a place that treats engineering as an investment — or toward executing known answers in a place that treats me as a cost?"**

---

## Applying This Framework

- **Status:** judge where the candidate sits on the relevant curve
- **Asset:** identify what is genuinely scarce about their experience
- **Runway:** read from the private profile. A comfortable runway justifies optimising for *direction over speed*.
- **Only deliberate work left:** (a) pick Pile-B / profit-centre / rising roles, (b) build influence meta-skill on top of technical depth
- **Motto:** Direction, not desperation
