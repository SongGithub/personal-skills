# Cover Letter Templates and Tailoring Guide

## Template: Standard 3-Paragraph

### Paragraph 1: Who I am & Why this role
> I'm a Senior/Lead Platform & DevOps engineer with [X] years experience building and running payments-scale systems on Kubernetes, AWS, and Terraform. I'm reaching out because [role title] at [company] aligns directly with the kind of work I do best — [specific overlap with JD].

### Paragraph 2: Relevant achievement (tailored)
> At [current employer], I [specific achievement most relevant to this role]. For example, when [specific challenge similar to what the role describes], I [action taken with measurable outcome]. This is directly relevant to [specific problem the hiring team faces based on JD].

### Paragraph 3: What I bring + call to action
> I'd bring a platform-engineering mindset — treating infrastructure as a product, building self-service capabilities, and compounding team productivity through automation and AI. I'd love to discuss how my experience aligns with [company]'s [specific priorities from JD]. Happy to make time for a chat.

## Customisation Rules
- Company name appears in every paragraph — shows you didn't copy-paste
- Reference 1-2 specific technologies from the JD
- Mention one concrete number or outcome from your experience
- Keep it under 250 words — hiring managers skim

## ATS / Portal Cover Letters
- Many portals have character limits (500-2000 chars)
- For textarea-based portals: shorten para 2 to one sentence, para 1 and 3 to 2 sentences each
- Don't waste characters on "I am writing to apply for..." — get straight to value
