# CV Impact Writing Skill

> A specialised skill for transforming CV bullets from responsibility-based (Level 1) to business-impact-based (Level 3) writing.

## The 3 Levels of CV Writing

### Level 1 — Responsibilities (Low Signal)
Describes what the candidate did. No differentiation.

*Example: "Managed AWS infrastructure for the payments team."*

### Level 2 — Quantified (Medium Signal)
Adds numbers and percentages. Better but numbers are hard to verify.

*Example: "Reduced AWS costs by 30% across 50+ instances."*

### Level 3 — Impact Writing (High Signal)
Connects the work to business outcomes. Signals business acumen.

*Example: "Redesigned CI/CD pipeline to cut deployment time from 4 hours to 15 minutes, enabling the payments team to ship 3x more features per sprint without growing platform headcount — directly supporting the company's UK expansion timeline."*

## The Core Test

For every bullet point, the candidate should be able to finish:
> "Because I did X, the business was able to Y."

If they can't, the bullet needs rewriting.

## Transformation Rules

When rewriting a bullet to Level 3, always answer three questions:

1. **Who benefited?** (team, company, customers, stakeholders)
2. **What changed for them?** (faster, cheaper, safer, more reliable, better experience)
3. **Why did it matter to the business?** (revenue, compliance, retention, capability, cost)

### Common transformations

| Level 1 → | Level 3 |
|---|---|
| "Led CI/CD migration" → "Freed the engineering team from manual release steps, reducing compliance response time from weeks to hours" |
| "Managed Kubernetes platform" → "Built a production K8s platform that onboarded 6 product teams, enabling infrastructure consolidation and reducing per-team ops overhead" |
| "Mentored junior engineers" → "Uplifted team DevOps capability through coaching, enabling self-service platform adoption without dedicated headcount" |
| "Led observability standardisation" → "Standardised observability across payments services, cutting incident response time and improving on-call quality for engineers" |

## Usage in Drafting

When generating a CV:

1. Write the initial bullet capturing the action and outcome
2. Audit against the three questions above
3. If any of the three questions are unanswered, rewrite until they are
4. Remove any bullet where the candidate cannot articulate the business impact truthfully
5. Prioritise bullets with the clearest business impact, not the most impressive-sounding technology

## Pitfalls

- **Do not fabricate business impact** — every claimed outcome must pass the interview backtrack test
- **Do not conflate team outcomes with personal outcomes** — "The team shipped faster" is different from "My changes enabled the team to ship faster"
- **Do not drop Level 3 writing just because the role is technical** — business impact is what differentiates senior/lead candidates
- **Keep it concise** — Level 3 bullets can be longer, but avoid wordiness
